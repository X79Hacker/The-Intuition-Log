# The Intuition Log

*A journey through the intuition, mathematics, theory, and implementation behind ideas in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.*

## About

The Intuition Log is a growing collection of my explorations across mathematics, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and robotics.

This repository documents my learning process through independent study, teaching, group discussions, mathematical explorations, and practical experimentation.

The objective is to go beyond memorizing definitions or applying existing methods. I want to understand the ideas behind them: the problems they address, the intuition that motivates them, the mathematics that describes them, and the mechanisms through which they work.

## What You'll Find Here

Depending on the topic, each collection may include:

- **Intuition:** Understanding concepts and the problems they address.
- **Mathematical Foundations:** Equations, derivations, proofs, and mathematical reasoning.
- **Theory:** Exploring the underlying principles, assumptions, and mechanisms.
- **Visual Explanations:** Diagrams, illustrations, and geometric interpretations.
- **Implementation:** Code, practical demonstrations, and experiments.
- **Observations & Insights:** Questions, limitations, connections, and ideas developed during exploration.

The materials may include PDF notes, handwritten explanations, mathematical derivations, visualizations, code experiments, and technical discussions.

### More Collections

Additional explorations across mathematics, probability and statistics, optimization, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and robotics will be added as the repository grows.

## Learning Philosophy

My approach to learning follows four guiding principles:

1. **Understand the intuition** behind an idea.
2. **Explore the mathematics** that supports it.
3. **Examine the theory** and understand how it works.
4. **Experiment with implementation** and practical applications.

Learning is an iterative process. Questions lead to exploration, exploration leads to understanding, and understanding often leads to better questions.

## Purpose

The Intuition Log is both a personal learning record and a resource for others interested in understanding the foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Robotics.

The intention is to make complex technical ideas more approachable through intuitive explanations, mathematical reasoning, visual exploration, and practical examples.

This repository will continuously evolve as I explore new concepts, revisit existing ideas, and develop deeper understanding.
