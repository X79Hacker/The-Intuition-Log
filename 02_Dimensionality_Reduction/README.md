# Dimensionality Reduction

An exploration of the mathematical, statistical, and geometric foundations of dimensionality reduction.

## Overview

Real-world datasets can contain hundreds or thousands of dimensions. Understanding the structure of such data can become challenging as dimensionality increases.

Dimensionality reduction explores methods for representing high-dimensional data in lower-dimensional spaces while preserving selected properties and relationships of the original data.

This collection focuses on understanding the intuition, mathematics, geometry, and practical behavior behind different dimensionality reduction techniques.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the meaning of dimensionality in data.
- Explore the challenges associated with high-dimensional spaces.
- Understand linear and nonlinear dimensionality reduction.
- Examine how relationships between data points are represented in lower-dimensional spaces.
- Investigate what information is preserved, distorted, or lost.
- Compare the assumptions, strengths, and limitations of different techniques.
